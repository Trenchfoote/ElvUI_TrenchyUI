# TrenchyUI Changelog

### New Features
- QoL: New Party Death Sound — plays a chosen sound when a group member dies in dungeons, raids, or the open world.

### Improvements
- BigWigs: The options window and keystone viewer now close with the Escape key.

### Bug Fixes
- QoL: The collected expansion button no longer drifts back onto the minimap on its own.
